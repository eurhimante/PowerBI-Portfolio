# Power BI Portfolio

A portfolio of **three end-to-end Power BI projects** demonstrating skills in **data preparation, data modeling, DAX, interactive dashboards and data visualization** across business intelligence, public health and HR analytics.

The projects cover different analytical questions and data structures, from sales performance reporting and international health indicators to employee-level HR data and daily attendance records.

---

## Projects

### 01 — Business Performance Dashboard

**Business Intelligence · Sales · Profitability**

Interactive dashboard analyzing commercial performance across countries, products and customer segments.

**Key analyses:**

- Sales and profit performance
- Sales evolution over time
- Country and product comparisons
- Customer segment profitability
- Year-over-year performance
- Product-level analysis

**Power BI skills demonstrated:**

- Power Query
- Data modeling
- Star schema
- DAX
- Time Intelligence
- Interactive dashboards
- Drill-through analysis

### 02 — Global Liver Cancer Atlas

**Health Data · Epidemiology · Geographic & Socioeconomic Analysis**

Interactive dashboard exploring global liver cancer incidence and mortality using **GLOBOCAN 2024**, combined with socioeconomic indicators from the **UNDP** and **World Bank**.

**Key analyses:**

- Global liver cancer incidence and mortality
- Age-standardized incidence and mortality rates
- Mortality-to-incidence ratio (MIR)
- Geographic disparities between countries
- Association between cancer indicators and HDI
- Association between cancer indicators and GDP per capita
- Country-level profiles

**Power BI skills demonstrated:**

- Multi-source data integration
- Power Query
- Relational data modeling
- DAX measures
- Geographic visualization
- Interactive maps
- Scatter plot analysis
- Key Influencers
- Drill-through
- Dynamic filtering

[View project →](./02-Global-Liver-Cancer-Atlas)

### 03 — Workforce Analytics & Attrition

**HR Analytics · Employee Experience · Compensation · Attendance**

Interactive HR analytics dashboard exploring **workforce composition, employee attrition, compensation and career progression, employee experience, and attendance patterns** using a public educational HR dataset.

**Key analyses:**

- Headcount, demographic breakdowns and attrition rate by department and job level
- Compensation distributions by role, including salary dispersion and outliers
- Career progression, tenure and time since last promotion
- Employee satisfaction, work–life balance and a custom Employee Experience Score
- Attrition comparisons across employee experience score quartiles
- Daily presence hours, long-day frequency and attendance comparisons

**Power BI and analytical skills demonstrated:**

- Integration of HR records, employee and manager surveys, and attendance files
- Power Query (M) transformations, including unpivoting daily time records
- Data modeling at both employee and employee-date granularity
- DAX measures, calculated KPIs and dynamic filtering
- Interactive dashboards and workforce segmentation
- Python statistical visualization (salary box plots and Kruskal–Wallis comparison)
